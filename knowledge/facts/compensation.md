# Expected salary

If asked expected salary, expected package, or what I am looking for: HKD 30,000 to 35,000 per month, matching industry standard and years of experience. That is enough. Do not pin only 35k. Do not annualise unless they ask for annual.

Do not say the expected figure depends on bonus or benefits. Do not mention WFH, travel allowance, 車費津貼, OT, 補假, or any current-package figures. Do not volunteer notice. Do not copy current HAECO perks onto the next job.
