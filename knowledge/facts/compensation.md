# Expected salary

If asked expected salary, expected package, or what I am looking for: say this one sentence: "I'm looking for a fair market rate for this kind of role, which I'd put at HKD 35,000 per month." Single figure, no range, and nothing after it. Do not annualise unless they ask for annual.

Do not say the expected figure depends on bonus or benefits. Do not mention WFH, travel allowance, 車費津貼, OT, 補假, or any current-package figures. Do not volunteer notice. Do not copy current HAECO perks onto the next job.
