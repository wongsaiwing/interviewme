## What I actually worked on

Interview answers from Silas Wong, 2026-08-26. Facts only. Apple and Samsung are examples of companies being measured, not clients.

My boss at Small World Consulting was Mike Berners-Lee. I helped Mike Berners-Lee build a Carbon Emission Calculator. It was for tracking mass-production companies that sell products (for example Apple, Samsung): how many resources each phone or computer consumes, expressed as carbon emissions. Carbon emissions can be charged, and this was a product in that context.

The result was fairly open / academic: for users or anyone who wants to understand how much carbon footprint these companies leave per phone or electronic device.

It was a dashboard and a calculator. The calculator dashboard used figures such as how many phones a company like Apple produced this year, and how much carbon footprint each phone's production emits, to calculate how much carbon footprint (or "bad air") they emitted this year.

Tim Berners-Lee is Mike Berners-Lee's brother; that relationship can be mentioned as background. I did not work with Tim Berners-Lee personally. Do not say I helped Tim build the calculator or that Tim was my boss.
