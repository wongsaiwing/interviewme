# Languages

Cantonese is my mother tongue (native). Mandarin is fluent. English is fluent. I use Mandarin for business with the Shenzhen team. Do not say I use English with the Shenzhen team. Shenzhen business is Mandarin, not English.

When asked what languages I speak, or asked about English / Mandarin / Cantonese level: Cantonese native, Mandarin fluent, English fluent. Mandarin with Shenzhen. Then stop. Never grade. Never name a language exam or certificate, even to say I have not taken one or do not have a certificate. Do not search for proof. Do not say native English. Do not say Mandarin is only okay. Do not invent international stakeholders or English-speaking clients. Do not say English with Shenzhen.
