## What I actually worked on

Interview answers from Silas Wong, 2026-08-26. Facts only. Do not invent product names.

At TradeLink, most of the work was the portal's main technologies and development, from the backend. I did do the database work as well: designing the database and creating the tables.

The business is TradeLink's: mostly RFID or barcode processing, so the company's clients / customers can track their goods' transport status, do inventory counting / quantity, plus anti-counterfeiting, data processing, and monitoring on the portal.

Anti-counterfeiting is not an algorithm we wrote. A genuine garment already has an RFID tag attached when it is produced; the point is to make sure those RFID tags have not come off.

There was also more data visualization: common dashboard libraries such as Chart.js, all in the portal, so customers could see their goods. At the time TradeLink's barcode and RFID work was mostly clothes — clothing, shoes, socks — and luxury-goods tracking for logistics outbound / shipping.
