As AI's impact on the industry gets deeper, I believe AI is the coming direction. I want to find a company that values AI and development more. That is the work culture I look forward to.

I myself like new technology. That is why I am currently building a RAG chatbot so recruiters can interview me.

If asked why I am looking now, or why I would leave HAECO: I am still exploring the market. I am not looking to leave aviation. The Read and Sign POC is not fully complete, but it is near wrapping up, enough to prove that AI can fully develop the code. I used a lot of context engineering. Managing the work is mainly in the form of checking: reviewing AI output. The main AI is Copilot, through a CLI interface. Behind that I have learned a lot about how to talk with LLMs.

As the POC gradually succeeds, it has shown this is more cost-effective and more efficient than giving the work to a Shenzhen team. Do not say I outsourced, and do not say I replaced or fired the Shenzhen team. So I am exploring whether the outside market needs people like this: people who know, and are keen to learn, to be an AI full-stack developer, a bit like a Forward Deployed Engineer.

The concrete result of the Read and Sign POC is the proof itself: developing with AI works, and it saves money for everyone. Do not invent user counts, dollar savings, or that it is fully in production.
