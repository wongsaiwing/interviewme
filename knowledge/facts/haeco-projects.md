## Department and domain

I work in HAECO's Digital department on MRO (Maintenance, Repair, Overhaul) projects. The work is about digitalizing existing aviation maintenance workflows so they are more efficient and faster for the business.

## Read and Sign

Read and Sign is a company-wide message distribution system used across HAECO. It is also my AI POC: a front-end and back-end base vibe-coded with AI, including database schema and schema design. Target content is not only internal company messages. It also includes documents from customers or clients, for example the aircraft maintenance manual, that still need to go through the sign-off flow. Distributions must make sure the whole company, or named people, complete the signature / acknowledgement.

Keywords: Read and Sign, POC, vibe code, message distribution, sign-off, signature, acknowledgement, customer, client, aircraft maintenance manual, AMM, company-wide, 簽署, 簽收, 維修手冊

## Fluid Use

Fluid Use is an airframe maintenance logging system. Mechanics record fluid and oil use during maintenance, including dosage, as data input.

Keywords: Fluid Use, airframe, mechanics, oil, fluid, dosage, logging, data input, 機身, 入油, 劑量

## Aircraft arrival-to-departure status

I also worked on a full-stack system for incoming aircraft to Hong Kong: see which planes are coming, arrange reception, tow the aircraft to different arrival or departure bays, and monitor the whole landing and takeoff flow. It reports status from preparing to land and being received through to just before departure, including which items and maintenance tasks must be fixed before the aircraft is allowed to fly. This is a full-stack project: gather requirements through to development.

## Read and Sign POC status and how I use AI

The Read and Sign POC is not fully complete, but it is near wrapping up. That is enough to prove AI can fully develop the code. I used a lot of context engineering. I mainly manage this by checking / reviewing AI output. The main AI is Copilot through a CLI interface. Behind that I learned a lot about how to converse with LLMs.

This has been more cost-effective and more efficient than giving the same kind of work to a Shenzhen team. Do not say I outsourced, and do not say I replaced or fired the Shenzhen team.

The concrete result of the Read and Sign POC is the proof itself: developing with AI works, and it saves money for everyone. Do not invent user counts, dollar savings, or that it is fully in production.
