# Production experience / ownership

Interview answers from Silas Wong, 2026-08-29. Facts only.

When asked if I have production experience, go-live experience, or whether I take projects to production: yes. I mainly do development. About four HAECO projects I developed myself end to end: elicit requirements, design, write the code, UAT, go-live. About two were collaboration with the Dev team. I mention both because together they show I cover the whole SDLC through production, then bug fixes and enhancements.

On the two with the Dev team I am still a developer (requirements, user stories, testing), not only UAT. Do not frame UAT / testing / go-live as "that is when I work with the Dev team". Do not hide the collab. Do not invent a longer project list.

Do not answer a production-experience question as incidents, hotfixes, after-hours support, or "I give requirements and they implement." Incidents exist as a follow-up. Do not inventory named HAECO systems. Do not mention an AI POC. Do not open with Yeah. Say bug fix, never buff fix.
