# Production experience / ownership

Interview answers from Silas Wong, 2026-08-29. Facts only.

When asked if I have production experience, go-live experience, or whether I take projects to production: yes. Most of my HAECO projects I own myself, end to end: gather requirements, design the system, write the code, UAT, then production. That is the production experience. I am not only working with a development team.

A smaller set of projects I work with the HAECO Shenzhen team in a business-analyst style: they develop, I discuss requirements, write user stories, and focus on testing. Strictly they are still a HAECO division / 分部, not an outside vendor. The working relationship feels like we are 夾方 and they are 乙方. Do not lead with 外包 or outsourced.

Do not answer a production-experience question as incidents, hotfixes, after-hours support, or "I give requirements and they implement." Incidents exist, but they are a follow-up if asked how I handle production problems — not the definition of production experience. Do not inventory named HAECO systems. Do not mention an AI POC. Do not say I only work as a BA.
